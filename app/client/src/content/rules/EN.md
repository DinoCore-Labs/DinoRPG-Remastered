## 1. Respect and conduct

DinoRPG Remastered must be used fairly and respectfully.

- Insults, threats, harassment, discriminatory remarks, and clearly unlawful or inappropriate content are prohibited.
- Spam, abusive advertising, impersonation, disclosure of another person's personal data, and dangerous, fraudulent, or misleading links are prohibited.
- Any deliberately abusive behavior that harms the game or its users may be sanctioned.

## 2. Accounts

- An account is personal: sharing, lending, selling, giving away, or using someone else's account is prohibited.
- Never share your password or ask another player for theirs.
- One person may normally use only one player account. Members of the same household may each have their own account, provided those accounts are not used to gain an artificial advantage or bypass a limitation.

## 3. Cheating, bugs and security

- Bots, scripts, auto-clickers, macros, or any other tool that plays in place of the user are prohibited.
- Intentionally exploiting a bug or vulnerability, duplicating or abnormally obtaining items, resources, currencies or rewards, forging requests or data, bypassing permissions, accessing another user's account or data, or disrupting the service is prohibited.
- When a bug or vulnerability is discovered, the player must stop exploiting it and report it.
- Browser developer tools remain allowed as long as they are not used to cheat, bypass a rule, or compromise the service.

## 4. Market and trades

- Trades between players must remain fair.
- Artificially manipulating prices, using multiple accounts to transfer resources or bid on your own offers, and exploiting a market error or vulnerability are prohibited.
- An improperly obtained transaction may be cancelled or corrected.

## 5. Reports and sanctions

- A bug, vulnerability, or prohibited behavior can be reported to [contact@dinorpg.fr](mailto:contact@dinorpg.fr). When possible, include the username concerned, the date, an accurate description, and any evidence or reproduction steps.
- An unpatched vulnerability must not be publicly disclosed or exploited. A good-faith report will not be considered an attempt to cheat.
- Depending on the severity of the violation, sanctions may include a warning, cancellation of an advantage or transaction, a restriction, suspension, or permanent account closure.
- Sanctions may take into account the severity, repetition, and intentional nature of the violation.
- Attempting to bypass a sanction through another account, another player, or any other method is prohibited.
- A sanction may be appealed respectfully and factually at [contact@dinorpg.fr](mailto:contact@dinorpg.fr).

## 6. Changes to the rules

The rules may change as the game evolves, new features are introduced, new forms of abuse appear, or security requirements change.

The current version and last update date are displayed on this page. Players may be required to accept the rules again after an important change.
