## 5. Personal Data and Cookies

The data controller is the non-professional publisher of DinoRPG Remastered. They can be contacted at the email address indicated below.

DinoRPG Remastered processes only the data necessary for account creation and management, game operation, progress saving, messaging, moderation, security, and support.

The username and password are required to create and secure the account. The email address is optional when it is not required by the registration form. Without an email address, certain features, particularly password recovery and account-related communications, may be unavailable.

- Account data: username, email address when provided, internal identifier, password stored in hashed form, role, and account creation and login dates.
- Game data: progress, Dinoz, inventory, virtual currencies, missions, scenarios, rankings, profile, and history of actions performed in the game.
- Communication data: private messages, participants, sending dates, read status, and any reports required for moderation.
- Technical data: IP address, connection information, server logs, technical errors, and actions required for service security.

This data is used to provide the requested service, authenticate players, save their progress, enable communications, provide support, enforce the rules, and protect the website against errors, abuse, and unauthorized access.

Processing necessary for the account, game, and messaging is based on the performance of the service requested by the player. Processing related to security, abuse prevention, and moderation is based on the publisher's legitimate interest in protecting the project and its users.

Data may be accessible to the publisher and authorized administrators when their intervention is necessary, as well as to technical service providers strictly necessary for hosting, security, and operation of the project. The main data is hosted on IONOS infrastructure. It is neither sold nor used for advertising purposes.

Account data, progress, and messages are retained for the lifetime of the account. After a deletion request, data is removed from the active database within a maximum of 30 days and progressively disappears from backups within a maximum of 90 days. Technical and security logs are retained for a maximum of 12 months. Support and moderation data is retained for a maximum of 12 months after the request or case is closed.

The website exclusively uses cookies and local storage necessary for its operation or for features expressly requested by the player:

- Authentication cookie: it keeps the player's session active and secures access to their account. Its maximum lifetime is seven days.
- Language preference: it stores the selected language on the device being used.
- Local game preferences: they store certain interface and animation settings selected by the player.

These trackers are not used for advertising, audience measurement, or tracking browsing on other websites. As they are strictly necessary for the service or requested preferences, they are not subject to prior consent.

The player can delete cookies and local data from their browser settings. Doing so may log them out and reset their preferences.

Each player may request access to, rectification of, or deletion of their data, as well as restriction of certain processing, data portability where applicable, or object to certain processing where permitted by law. The request must make it possible to identify the account concerned, for example using the username and associated email address.

To exercise these rights or ask a question about personal data: [contact@dinorpg.fr](mailto:contact@dinorpg.fr)

A complaint may also be submitted to the [Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL)](https://www.cnil.fr).
