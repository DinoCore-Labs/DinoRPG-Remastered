## Why can't Grandpa Joe's last two missions be unlocked?

The 'Giants' mission will be unlocked when your Dino reaches level 11 and the 'Achievement of the Month' when he reaches level 18.

## Where's Mrs Ducraft's dog?

Mrs. Ducraft's Dog is located at the Preaching Port, accessible from the Fountain of Youth. The Fountain of Youth is accessible from Dinotown, Brutforce, or Papy Joe's.

## Where is the market of the Atlantean Islands where I need to exchange a banana for a coat rack (second mission of Bao Bob, 'The barter of the Atlantean Islands')?

This mission is done in stages. You will need to go to the Coral Mines, then to the Waikiki Islands, then to the Mutant Falls, then to the Sticky Swamp, back to the Coral Mines, and finally to Mr. Bao Bob's.

In each of these places, you must talk to a character and make the exchange they propose.

## How to get more than 18 Dinoz?

The U Leader skill :bois: allows you to have 3 more Dinoz on your account.
The same goes for :air: Messiah on the new 50+ skills tree.

U (unique) skills only work once per account.
After that, it's possible to have many more by freezing some of your Dinoz.

## What to choose in the game of Heads or Tails against Yakuzi?

Regardless of the choice, the first bet will inevitably be lost while the second will inevitably be won.

## How does the Fountain Pearl work?

Any Dinoz at the Fountain of Youth at midnight will gain 5 :pv: per day upon your daily login. The effect is not cumulative.

## How to obtain the Fountain Pearl?

You will need the Buoy.
A partir du Port de Prêche, accédez à l'Île Waïkiki (Îles Atantéinées), puis accédez aux chutes Mutantes (accessibles depuis le Dôme Soulaflotte, Chez Bao Bob, et depuis le Marais Collant).

Talk to Master Hydargol, and follow his instructions. If you successfully complete the mission he gives you, you will obtain the Fountain Pearl.

## How to remove a skill from a Dinoz?

Learned skills cannot be removed, but you can disable certain ones using the 130-lesson Strategy Guide you will obtain by completing "The Archelionscarer" - the second mission of the Soft Shaman.

As a general rule, avoid choosing attacks (A skills) in the minor elements of your Dinoz.
For example, Water Cannon is not recommended for a softpig.

## How to obtain the Lantern?

Tout d'abord, vous devez avoir le Gant des Zors afin d'accéder à la forêt de Grumhel par les Îles Atlantéinées.

Go to the Haunted Hills and talk to the Strange Man. Ignore him and fight a monster.
The Lantern allows you to go to the Deep Gorges and complete the Hieroglyphs mission of the Soft Shaman.

## Where can I find the dictionary I asked for in Soft Shaman's first mission, 'The trial'?

The dictionary is at the University.

## Can I have my Dinoz fight together?

You cannot have them fight against each other, but you can have them fight alongside each other. To group your Dinoz, they must be in the same location and each have an available action.

Select the action 'Follow': your Dinoz are now in a team. You can group up to three Dinoz; the limit can increase to five if the leading Dinoz of the group has learned the skills :bois: Sympathy and Charisma.

## I've completed Grandpa Joe's missions, how can I get more?

At the Atlantean Islands, Mr. Bao Bob (At Mr. Bao Bob's) will offer you a new list of missions. Au Grand Tout Chaud, c'est le Shaman mou (Fosselave) qui a besoin de vos services.

## How to access the Magnetic Steppes?

You or the members of your clan, if you have one, must gather 7 Dinoz at Bao Bob's (in posession of the Korgon Fins which is mandatory).

Once the 7 Dinoz are concentrating, you will be granted access to a portal that will take you to a world where, after a little journey and defeating a big guardian, you will obtain a key.

This is the key to the Blacksylva Gate and it will grant you access to the Magnetic Steppes... Accessible from the Grumhel forest.

Advice: primarily intended for high-level Dinoz, around level 30 and above... Dinoz with Water as their main element are at disadvantage.

To create a clan: you must have at least 20,000:gold: and 15 points.

## How to obtain the Zors Glove? How do I get to the Gruhmel Forest?

Firstly, you'll need the Buoy, Climbing Gear, Shovel, and Diver's Mask.
With the Shovel, dig at the Basalt Slopes (Hot Mounts), the Sticky Marsh (Atlantean Islands), and the Fountain of Youth (Dinotown). You should obtain the statuses 'Marsh Mud', 'Granite Shard', and 'Pure Water'.

Next, go to the Underwater Dome and talk to the Archisage, then give him your collected items. Après avoir combattu l'Élementaire de bois, vous obtiendrez le Gant des Zors. Now head to the Mutant Falls.

I'm unsure if you already need to have the Fountain Pearl, but it's preferable. Talk to Master Hydargol at the Mutant Falls and agree to lend him the Zors Glove. You will receive a Lily Pad Leaf. Now, you can access the Grumhel forest from the Mutant Falls.

With the Scorpscuba Caller from the Mutant Falls.

To obtain the mask, you will need the Buoy and a fairly strong Dinoz. Go to the Atlantean Islands from the Preach Port, and head to the Mutant Falls. Talk to the Atlantean Guard, he will tell you about a fisherman friend at the Preach Port. So, return to the Frish Port and talk to Jovébozé.

He will take you to fight a Diver, and if you manage to kill it, he will give you a Scorpscuba Caller.

To obtain a relic, you need to dig at the Ashpouk Ruins and then go to the university to authenticate the relic.

Pour l'obtenir, il faut d'abord : la Bouée, le Matériel d'escalade et la Pelle. Minimum levels for the Buoy and Climbing Gear: 5 and 6.

## How to access the Grumhel forest from the Deep Gorges or how to access the second part of the Grumhel forest?

You will need the Zors Glove, the Lily Pad, and the Buoy. Go to the Mutant Falls, and move to the Grumhel Forest (Location 'Go pass the Mutant Falls'). From there, go to the Haunted Hills. Talk to the Weird Man and ignore him. He will then tell you about a horrible monster, and ask to fight it. You will then enter into combat with a Korgon Prankster. If you manage to defeat it (which is quite possible), the Weird Man will flee, leaving you with his lantern. Now, leave the Grumhel Forest, leave the Atlantean Isles, leave Dinoland and go to the Big All-Hot Mountain, to the Tunnel under the Branch. From there, access the Deep Gorges (Move action). From the Deep Gorges, access the Korgon Camp (Move action). You are now at the Korgon Camp, in the second part of the Grumehl Forest. To join the two sides, do Dian Korgsey's missions, and obtain the Korgon Fins. Now, you have the possibility to move from the Korgon Camp to the Jumin River, and vice versa.

## Dark Dinoz and Hippoclamp, Pteroz and Rocky trophies

The Dark Hippoclamp can be found on Waikiki Island, accessible from the Frish Port via the Buoy. Defeating it will earn you the Seahorse Trophy. And you need to be at least level 8 to see it.

The Dark Pteroz can be found on the Basalt Slopes, accessible from the Climbing Hills with the Climbing Equipment. You'll get the Pteroz Trophy by beating it.

The Rocky is in BrutForce. It isn't visible until level 13 and is difficult to defeat until level 15, but it will earn you the Rocky Trophy.

Trophies are epic items that can be seen on your account. Ils vous permettent d'acheter dans l'Enclos le dinoz correspondant au Trophée : Hippoclamp si vous avez le Trophée Hippoclamp, etc.

## How do you freeze a Dinoz?

Go to Deep Gorges and talk to the spelunker. He'll offer to freeze your Dinoz.
This will allow you to raise an extra Dinoz.

Of course, you'll need the lantern to access the gorges.

## How do I get a Kabuki?

To obtain this precious dinoz, you'll need the Kabuki aura, which you can get by completing Yakuzi's quest!

To begin with, you'll need to meet Yakuzi at the Frish Port (a random encounter), and accept his two bets.
He will then disappear, and you can meet him again, first at the Forges, then at Deep Gorges.
During the latter encounter, you will obtain a parchment, which you will have to have translated at the University by a professor.
The professor will tell you that you need to give him a number of ingredients to advance your quest.
Once you've been given these ingredients, and after waiting a few days, you'll have to go to the Coral Mines (you may have to wait a little while for a tab to appear, like for a mission stage).
Pour terminer cette quête, vous devrez poursuivre votre assaillant sur l'Île du Totem, qui n'est pas accessible avant.

You'll also get a Kabuki Egg, and the Kabuki Aura, which will allow you to visit this place as often as you like.

## How do I sell my Dinoz?

To be able to sell your Dinoz, you must already own two (you can't stay without a Dinoz). Then go to the Market Place, click on Breeders' Market and then on Sell.
WARNING: this operation is irreversible.
