# The Missions

During your adventures in Dinoland, you will encounter many characters. Some of these characters, like **GrandPa Joe**, will grant you access to a list of **Missions**.

Each Mission has a specific objective :

![The Missions](asset://guide/missions)

Your Dinoz can only undertake one mission at a time, so choose carefully which one you want to undertake and then complete it. Once the mission's objective is fulfilled, you can return to the character who assigned the mission to your Dinoz to claim a reward.

Some missions are initially locked. To unlock them, you will need to complete other missions first, increase your Dinoz's level, or finish certain **Quests** given by other characters.

Once a mission is completed by one Dinoz, it can be undertaken again by another of your Dinoz.
