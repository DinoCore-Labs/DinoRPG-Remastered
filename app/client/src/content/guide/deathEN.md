# Death

It can happen that while fighting a monster that is too strong or because it did not have enough life, your Dinoz dies during a battle. While it is dead, your Dinoz cannot perform **any actions**. It is therefore immobilized until it is resurrected.

You have 2 options to **resurrect** your Dinoz :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **For free**: your Dinoz will return to Dinotown and will lose half of its accumulated experience points since its last level.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) With an **angel potion**: your Dinoz will remain where it is and will not lose any experience points.

Depending on how you prefer to manage your gold coins :gold:, either solution can be interesting.
