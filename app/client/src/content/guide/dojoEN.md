# Dojos

**Dojos** are places where you can train your Dinoz against each other in single combat.

- ![](asset://act/act_train) You can have your Dinoz fight each other there, but also challenge the Dinoz of other clan members.

You are offered a maximum of 50 challenges every day, in sets of 5, and it's up to you to win them to raise your Dojo in the rankings.

If you win or cancel the 5 challenges offered, a new list of 5 challenges will immediately be proposed to you (within the limit of remaining challenges).

## ...Creating your Dojo :

To create your Dojo, you will need to meet Master Zenit who is often found at Forcebrut. He will explain how to build your Dojo and how it works. You will need to choose a location and click on the Dojo button in the left menu. Once the Dojo is built, you can access it from anywhere.

## ...Challenges :

Challenges are battles in which you will fight against another player's Dinoz. You will be asked to defeat this opponent, whether or not you aim to achieve a pre-set objective.

- ![](asset://act/act_defi) This button allows access to the Dojo challenges. Choose an opponent from the list of proposed challenges! Then select the Dinoz you deem most suitable to win while fulfilling the objective.

## ...Inter-Dojo Tournament :

The Tournament is a monthly event in DinoRPG, bringing together 256 dojos that qualified during the elimination phases into 16 groups. They will battle each other until determining the best player in Dinoland!

- ![](asset://act/act_tournament) This button allows access to the Tournament page. For over a week, players will compete in 16 groups of 16 players. Once the first round is over, the 16 finalists will compete in the final group. The best Master Dinoz will be determined after this challenge. _As with challenges, during the final phases, Dinoz do not lose their health points. But this time, the battles will be more varied, with or without magical items, 1-on-1 or in teams: 3-on-3, 5-on-5. This will vary depending on the editions!_

On the Tournament page, you can select the Dinoz that will participate in the Tournament; you cannot change them between matches in the final phases. The tournament begins when the elimination phases end.

Players who qualify for a tournament cannot participate in the next tournament. However, they can try their luck in the following edition.

## ...Combat History :

- ![](asset://act/act_history) This button allows access to the challenges you have completed, so you can review these battles at your leisure.

## ...Miscellaneous Questions :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **Is creating a Dojo free?** No, creating your Dojo will cost you a certain amount of gold coins. But don't worry, your daily battles earning you gold, it won't take you long to be able to create your Master buildings.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **How to customize your Dojo?** Did you notice the button? Then you must have noticed it's not active at the moment... Indeed, we are working on this feature, so a little patience.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **What is the frequency of the Inter-Dojo Tournaments?** Every month, following the previous tournament's final, a new qualification phase begins. It always ends on the last day of the month at midnight, followed by the elimination phases for the 256 qualified dojos in the following days.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **How to participate in the Inter-Dojo Tournament?** To participate in the Tournament, you must be one of the top 256 Dojos of the month. The ranking lets you know the level of your opponents. Players who qualify for the final phases will receive a reward.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **How to win a Toufufu egg?** Your Dojo must be among the top 50 in the Dojo rankings at the end of the month to win this reward.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **I'm not here every day, how do I complete all my challenges?** If you don't do your 50 daily fights, they will carry over to the next day without limit.
