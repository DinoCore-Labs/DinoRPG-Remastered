# Questions?

If you have questions, issues, or suggestions for the DinoRPG team, please take the time to read the different responses offered below :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **Who created DinoRPG ?** Motion Twin! The independent studio was founded in 2001 and has created over 150 web games. Today, Motion Twin owes its resurgence to Dead Cells, a game released in 2017 on PC, consoles, and smartphones.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **Why was the game stopped by Motion Twin ?** Motion Twin stopped developing their online games when Adobe discontinued updates for Flash Player in 2020. Motion Twin ceased all of its web games and Twinoid in November 2023.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **But is DinoRPG still here ?** Yes, thanks to Eternaltwin. This project, launched by a few players, decided to preserve these games and prevent the complete disappearance of this common video game heritage.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **Is DinoRPG free ?** Yes, DinoRPG is a free game.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **How can I get more gold coins?** Your Dinoz can earn gold coins every day by battling or completing missions. In addition, every day, you will obtain a Daily Ticket that will either give you gold or a Box that contains various objects. Or simply, gather ingredients and sell them to the Wandering Merchant.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) **How to restart the game?** In case of a problem, you can restart the game from the beginning. To do this, simply go to the **My Account / Edit my Info** section to reset your account.
