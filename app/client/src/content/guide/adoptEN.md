# Adopting a Dinoz

The first thing you need to do on DinoRPG is to adopt a Dinoz. For this, you have **200,000** :gold: (gold coins) obtained when creating your account.

By clicking on **BUY A DINOZ** in the left Menu, you can access the Dinoz Shop. Each Dinoz is unique, and every day, this shop will offer you a selection of Dinoz among which you can choose the one that suits you the most.

Each Dinoz is presented as follows :

![Adopting a Dinoz](asset://guide/adopt)

You can see the look of the Dinoz, its **Race** and its Price. There are different breeds of Dinoz, each with its own properties. In particular, the values of the **5 elements** indicated during the purchase depend on the breed of the Dinoz, and some breeds have an **additional skill**. These two points influence the **fight** as we will see later. The price also varies depending on the breed of the Dinoz, some breeds being rarer and therefore more expensive.

First, choose the Dinoz you prefer and buy it.
