# Clan Wars

Clans can also clash with each other during the Clan War! Here are the principles and rules :

## ...The Clan's Treasury :

Each Clan has a Treasury that corresponds to the value of the available ingredients in the Clan.

To fill the Clan's Treasury, each member can donate all or part of their collected ingredients during their gathering actions. From the Ingredients page in the right menu, you will have access to a button allowing you to 'Give to the Clan'.

The treasury has different uses, here they are :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) _Building/rebuilding the Castle_
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) _Declaring war against another Clan_
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) _Paying the tax collector_

## ...The Clan's Castle :

Each Clan can build its own Castle from the **'Castle** tab. To build it, you need ingredients that will be withdrawn from your Treasury. Only the Clan leader can initiate the first construction of the Castle.

Once the castle is built, it is possible to assign defenders to it who will ensure the protection of the castle during enemy attacks. To assign one of your dinoz to defend the castle, you need to go to the location of your castle and perform the action **'Defend the castle'**.

If all defenders are killed in combat or if there are no more defenders, the castle will suffer damage and will therefore be damaged.

## ...Declaring war :

This tab allows you to consult all information about wars such as the clans you are attacking, the clans attacking you, and the battles you have already won against opposing clans.

To start a war, you need to have sufficient ingredients in your treasury. A war lasts a maximum of 100 hours and cannot be canceled. To win the war, you will need to destroy the enemy castle.

## ...Attacks :

To attack a castle, you will need to locate it, so you will need to move your Dinoz until you find the enemy castle, after which you simply press the 'Attack' button and you will see your group of Dinoz challenge the defenders of the castle, or in their absence, attack the castle directly.

These assaults have a time limit to fight, if you defeat the defenders within this time frame, all your Dinoz will attack the enemy castle.

By destroying the enemy castle, you will win the war, while if your castle is destroyed, you will lose.

## ...Defense :

When your Clan is attacked, it is important to prepare a good defense in order not to lose the War launched by an opposing clan or when you declare war.

The Clan leader, as well as members with the necessary rights, will be able to adjust the defense by moving the Dinoz to adapt the best strategy to prevent your Castle from being hit.

## ...The Tax Collector :

The tax collector must be paid before declaring an attack. He always passes within the half-hour following the hour he proposes! For example, if he says he will pass from 16:24 to 17:50, you can pay from 16:00 to 16:30.
