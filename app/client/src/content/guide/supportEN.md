# Technical Support

## ...Clearing your cache :

Before anything else, consider trying *_to clear the cache_ of your web browser! This simple operation can solve many technical problems.

On Microsoft Edge :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Press the [Ctrl], [Shift] and [Del] keys at the same time
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) A new window will open. In the tab, select 'Cached images and files'.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Confirm your choice by clicking on the 'Clear' button.

On Mozilla Firefox :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Click on the menu button and select Settings.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Select the Privacy & Security panel.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) In the Cookies and Site Data section, click on the 'Clear Data...'
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Check the 'Cached Web Content' box, then click on the 'Clear' button.

On Google Chrome :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Click on 'More', and choose 'Clear browsing data'.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Select 'Cached images and files' and click on 'Clear data'.
