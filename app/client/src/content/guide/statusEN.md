# Statuses

By completing various **Missions/Quests**, your Dinoz will be able to obtain numerous **statuses** that will be listed on its profile. Hover over one of these statuses to get help on its effect.

Generally, statuses are permanent. This means that once obtained, you cannot lose them. However, some statuses are temporary and can therefore be lost.

The statuses are displayed when you view another player's Dinoz: will you be able to view the maximum of them?
