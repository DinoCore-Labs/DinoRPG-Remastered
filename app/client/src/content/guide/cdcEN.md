# Clan Championship

**Clans** can also compete against each other during the Clan Championship! Here are the principles and rules:

## ...The Championship :

Once a championship has started, no Clan can join a ranking or change its ranking until the end of this Championship.

It is not possible to have a tie score during the final phase: if this were to happen, one of the two Clans would be declared the winner by drawing lots.

## ...Battle :

A Battle is launched when two clans face each other. A battle lasts for _3 days_, and at the end, the Clan with the highest **score** wins.

A Battle takes place at a specific location. You will need to move your Dinoz to this location for them to participate. A player can have up to _3 Dinoz_ in a Battle, and in any case, a Clan cannot have more than _45 Dinoz_ in a Battle.

Here is a simplified Battle view:

![Clan Championship](asset://guide/battle_cdc)

At the top, you can see the current **score** of the Battle, and at the bottom, the remaining time before the end of the Battle.

Dinoz that have joined the Battle can move one square, then they must wait for a certain number of minutes before they can move again. It is possible to accumulate actions if you do not use them immediately.

You can click on a Dinoz to move it or to know who it belongs to. Only the owner of a Dinoz can move it.

_We remind you that sharing accounts between players is prohibited, and in case of violation of the rules, all involved accounts will be permanently blocked._

Good organization among Clan members is therefore necessary to win the victory.

## Rating

As you can see on the Battle display, each Clan has **a score line**, blue for the Clan on the left and red for the Clan on the right.

Every _15 minutes_, each Clan scores as many points as the advancement of its score line. Thus, in the example above, the Clan on the left (blue line) will score 2 points and the Clan on the right (red line) 4 points.

The score line is positioned as follows: it indicates the position of the first Dinoz of the Clan that has not been surpassed by a Dinoz of the opposing Clan.

**Warning!** An opposing Dinoz can thus make a breakthrough that will push back your score line, preventing you from scoring points in the next calculation.

Reminder: a Battle lasts for _3 days_, and at the end, the Clan with the highest **score** wins.

## ...Fights :

If a Dinoz tries to move to the location occupied by an enemy Dinoz, a fight ensues. All Dinoz within 10 squares around the two combatants participate in the fight, according to the following scheme :

![Clan Championship](asset://guide/position_cdc)

The placement of Dinoz is therefore extremely important if you do not want to find yourself at a numerical disadvantage during a fight.

If a Dinoz attacks an opponent from behind, it does so alone, the opponent will be taken by surprise and will not be able to call for reinforcements. The fight will then be a 1 versus 1.

If a Dinoz dies during a fight, it is removed from the Battle and cannot rejoin. A Dinoz can also voluntarily leave the Battle by returning to their Clan's sidelines; they can then heal and return to battle.
