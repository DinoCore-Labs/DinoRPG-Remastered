# Dinoz Profile

The **Profile** of your Dinoz is where you can both see the **state** of your Dinoz and perform a number of **actions**. It is presented as follows:

![Dinoz Profile](asset://guide/card)

You can observe the following information :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) At the top right, the **elements** and **race** of your Dinoz.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Below the image, in the red circle, its **level**, indicating its current power. In the bar marked :pv: (HP), its **hit points**: this is your Dinoz's health. When the bar is completely empty, your Dinoz **dies**. In the bar marked :xp: (XP), its **experience points**: you gain them after each battle. When the bar is full, your Dinoz can progress to the next level.
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Below, the different **actions** your Dinoz can perform, such as Fighting.

We will now discuss the various actions your Dinoz can perform.
