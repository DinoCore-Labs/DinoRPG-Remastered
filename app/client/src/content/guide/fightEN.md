# Fights

A battle occurs when your Dinoz is attacked or attacks one or more monsters. The different participants then join the battle, which unfolds automatically :

![Fights](asset://guide/fight)

Monsters and your Dinoz take turns attacking, based on their **initiative**, **speed**, and **energy**. With each hit, the opponent loses **health points**, which are displayed. Your Dinoz must defeat all the monsters to win the battle.

During its turn, your Dinoz can perform one or more of the following actions based on its energy :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Launch an **assault**, meaning a normal attack
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Execute a **special attack**, which then replaces the assault
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Use an **event** type skill
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) Use **combat equipment**

## The Elements

A Dinoz has 5 **elemental** values that are indicated on its profile:

- ![](asset://elements/elem_fire) Fire
- ![](asset://elements/elem_wood) Wood
- ![](asset://elements/elem_water) Water
- ![](asset://elements/elem_lightning) Lightning
- ![](asset://elements/elem_air) Air

These elements are organized according to **the Grand Cycle of elements**:

![Fights](asset://guide/elements)

An element is strong against the two that follow it and weak against the two that precede it. Thus, for example, Fire is very strong against Wood and quite strong against Water, but is very weak against Air and rather weak against Thunder.

## The Attacks

Assaults always occur in a specific order, determined by the elemental values, with a random draw in case of a tie.

![Fights](asset://guide/assault)

Thus, a Dinoz with the above elements will perform its assaults in the following order :

- ![](asset://icons/info_button) :water: Water first
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) then :wood: Wood
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) then :foudre: Thunder and :air: Air in an undetermined order
- ![](asset://icons/info_button) and finally :feu: Fire

Once the 5 assaults are completed, the Dinoz will start the cycle again.

Based on its **elements** and **skills**, the Dinoz has a certain **assault power** and **defense** for each element. These characteristics are visible in the Details tab of the **Dinoz's profile**.

The stronger **the assault power** of an element, the more hit points the Dinoz will deduct from its opponents when performing an assault of that element. The stronger **the defense** against an element, the more protected the Dinoz will be against attacks from opponents using that element.

## The Monsters

Many monsters perform Void element assaults. This means that all your elements are taken into account during defense. However, some monsters are capable of performing assaults or special attacks of a particular element.

## Gains

At the end of the battle, your Dinoz earns **gold coins** :gold: that will allow it to heal, and **experience points** that will enable it to level up.

## The Energy

![Fights](asset://guide/energy)

Each Dinoz has a blue energy bar next to its health bar. This bar represents the **energy** that the Dinoz possesses, and it is halfway filled at the beginning of the battle. Like the health bar, it depends on the maximum energy called **endurance** that the Dinoz possesses. _The endurance of a Dinoz can vary depending on certain learned skills. Bonuses can also increase endurance._

Each skill has an energy cost. With each skill used, the energy bar decreases. Once empty, the Dinoz must pass its turn. Some extraordinarily powerful skills require much more energy than others.

This energy bar gradually fills up during the battle, referred to as **recovery**. _The recovery of a Dinoz can vary depending on certain learned skills._

The Dinoz must therefore wait until it has replenished its energy before using a skill.
