# Healing

Once a **battle** is finished, your Dinoz may have lost some **hit points**. Therefore, it is necessary to heal it to prevent it from dying. You can purchase various items from the **Shop** with your gold coins :gold: for this purpose.

Access the **Inventory tab** from your Dinoz's profile to see the list of items you possess and to be able to use them. For example, a **Cloud Burger** will restore 10 hit points to your Dinoz.

![Healing](asset://guide/heal)

## ...Resting :

If your Dinoz has low hit points, it can **Rest**. This allows it to automatically recover hit points over time. However, it cannot perform any actions while resting.

Initially, your Dinoz will recover at a rate of **1** hit point per hour, but this can be improved by certain skills.

You can stop your Dinoz's rest at any time, but your Dinoz cannot regain more than **half** of its hit points while resting.
