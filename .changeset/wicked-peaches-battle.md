Add the Dinoland Clinic scenario and collaborative clan actions system.

### Highlights

- Introduce collaborative clan construction projects, with shared progress, action contributions, and completion tracking.
- Add the Dinoland Clinic construction project, requiring 600 clan actions to unlock its services.
- Implement Anna Tomie's storyline, dialogues, and seven clinic missions.
- Add free Dinoz healing with a one-hour treatment duration and a 20-hour cooldown per player.
- Introduce cosmetic surgery with randomized visual changes, a one-hour duration, and a 30-day cooldown per Dinoz.
- Persist clinic treatments, surgeries, and clan construction progress using Prisma.
- Add clinic countdowns, interface improvements, and translations in French, English, Spanish, and German.
- Improve Dinoz group handling, gameplay protections, and concurrent action safety.
- Add unit and integration tests covering clinic services, missions, clan contributions, and progression.
